Segment Stitch

Asks TheIntroDB and IntroDB and reads the item's named chapters, then merges intros, recaps, credits and previews per type. Disable the TheIntroDB, Chapter Segments and Gelato IntroDB providers in the library settings, or their segments are kept instead.

Sources

Weighs your own submissions higher and raises the rate limits.
IntroDB only takes IMDb ids and numbers, so it is skipped for series with a non-default display order.

Segment types

Merging

Smart keeps the overlap (latest start to earliest end) of the most sources that overlap, leaves out a source that overlaps none of the others, and gives no segment when two sources disagree completely. It errs on skipping too little.
Comma separated, from TheIntroDb, IntroDb, Chapters. The order Priority picks by, and the tie-break of Smart.

Refresh

An item's segments are fetched again after this many days (give or take a quarter), so corrections arrive. 0 never refreshes. A source with nothing for an item is asked again after 14 days.
Otherwise new items get their segments on the next media segment scan.
